Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information is collected on Karnata.in and how it is protected.

Overview

At Karnata.in, we believe in privacy by default. We do not require account registration, logins, or personal profile creation to access our public directories, market price calculators, or map services.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

Karnata.in uses Google AdSense to display third-party advertisements that help keep our public data services free for all users.

  • Google and its advertising partners use cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this website and other websites on the internet.
  • These cookies help ensure ads are relevant and prevent the same ad from being displayed repeatedly.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also configure your web browser to reject cookies or notify you when cookies are being set.

Local Storage Usage

When you select a preferred location (such as a specific district or GBA ward) on Karnata.in, this setting is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This ensures you don't have to re-enter your location on every visit. This information stays on your device and is never sold or transmitted to third parties.

Log Files & Analytics

Like almost all websites, our hosting servers generate standard access logs. These contain technical details such as your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamp. This data is used strictly for server health monitoring, security, and traffic performance analysis.

Children's Privacy

Karnata.in does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details through our contact form, please notify us at contact@karnata.in and we will promptly remove it.
