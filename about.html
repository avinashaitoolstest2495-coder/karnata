About Karnata.in

An independent digital platform connecting citizens, farmers, students, and travelers with verified daily public information across Karnataka.

Who We Are

Karnata.in was created to solve a simple problem: finding practical, everyday public data for Karnataka in one clean, fast, and easy-to-use place.

Whether you want to check today's APMC mandi commodity prices in Kolar or Shivamogga, check current water levels across major dams like KRS and Almatti, find your local Gram Panchayat PDO contact, or understand the new Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) ward boundaries, we bring this information together in clear Kannada and English.

What We Provide

  • Agricultural Market Prices (APMC): Daily wholesale price updates for key crops (paddy, tomato, onion, arecanut, coconut, cotton) across major mandis in Karnataka.
  • Water & Reservoir Tracking: Daily storage metrics, inflows, and outflows for major reservoirs across the Cauvery and Krishna river basins.
  • Commodities & Fuel Rates: Accurate daily retail benchmarks for 22K/24K Gold, Silver, Petrol, and Diesel across Karnataka districts.
  • Elected Representatives & Governance: Profiles and constituency records for all 224 Karnataka MLAs, 28 MPs, and 5,935 Gram Panchayats.
  • Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) Portal: Interactive boundary maps and directory for the 5 City Corporations and 369 delimited wards in Bengaluru.

Where Our Data Comes From

We believe in transparency. We do not generate fictional numbers. All statistical feeds and public directories on Karnata.in are compiled from verified public domain sources, including:

  • Agmarknet (Government of India Ministry of Agriculture) for APMC mandi rates
  • Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre (KSNDMC) & Water Resources Dept for dam storage
  • India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA) for gold benchmarks
  • Election Commission of India (ECI) for legislative election data
  • Karnataka Rural Development & Panchayat Raj (RDPR Panchatantra 2.0) for Gram Panchayat contacts

Editorial Standards & Corrections

We strive to keep every record accurate and up to date. If you notice an error in any ward, representative contact, or data table, please reach out to us at contact@karnata.in. We verify and correct reported inaccuracies promptly.
